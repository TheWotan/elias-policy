Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the app ELIAS handles user information.

Information We Collect

We do not collect personal data such as your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, or precise location.

The app stores limited app data on your device for normal functionality, such as:

  • game progress and local app state
  • package unlock and purchase status
  • basic technical information required by Apple to process in-app purchases

How Information Is Used

Any data handled by the app is used only to:

  • provide core app functionality
  • save your progress locally on your device
  • unlock or restore in-app purchases

Third-Party Services

The app uses Apple services for App Store distribution and in-app purchases.

  • Apple App Store
  • Apple In-App Purchases

These services may process data according to Apple's own privacy policies.

Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal information.

We do not share personal data with advertisers or data brokers.

Data Retention

Data stored by the app is kept locally on your device unless you remove the app or clear its data.

Your Rights

Depending on your region, you may have the right to:

  • ask questions about how the app handles data
  • request information about any personal data processing, if applicable

Children's Privacy

The app does not knowingly collect personal data from children.
