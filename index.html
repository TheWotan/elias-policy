Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how the app ELIAS handles user information.

How Information Is Used

Any data processed by the app is used only to:

  • restore purchases, if applicable

Third-Party Services

The app may use third-party services such as:

These services may process data according to their own privacy policies.

Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal information.

We may share limited technical data only with service providers strictly necessary to operate the app.

Data Retention

We keep data only for as long as necessary to provide the app's functionality, comply with legal obligations, or resolve disputes.

Your Rights

Depending on your region, you may have the right to:

  • request deletion of your data
  • ask questions about how your data is handled

Children's Privacy

The app is not intended to knowingly collect personal data from children.
